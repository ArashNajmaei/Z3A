§1The practice

Most advice is either academically sound or commercially useful. We are built to be both.

Our team teaches and research at postgraduate level, and bring more than a decade of industry work in analytics, strategy consulting and market research. That combination shapes everything we deliver: evidence first, frameworks that hold up to scrutiny, and outputs people can actually use on Monday morning.

We work in two worlds. For education providers, we build and deliver curriculum that meets accreditation expectations and engages students. For organisations, we provide strategic and analytics advice grounded in current research rather than recycled templates.

§4How an engagement runs

Diagnose

A short scoping conversation, then a review of your context, data and constraints. We agree the question before we answer it.

Design

We set out the approach, the deliverables and the timeline in a written proposal with a fixed scope.

Deliver

Work is produced in reviewable stages, so you see drafts early and steer the direction.

Embed

Handover includes documentation and, where useful, a session that equips your people to carry the work forward.
